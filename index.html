Political Campaigner & Advocate

Sherrie De Kiguchi — Hampshire campaigner fighting for a safer, fairer Britain

Born and raised in Hampshire, Sherrie De Kiguchi is a British political campaigner, parish councillor, and former finance professional. A frontline charity worker supporting rape survivors, she now campaigns publicly against child sexual exploitation (CSE) and grooming gangs, and for justice for victims, public safety and immigration reform across Britain.

About Sherrie De Kiguchi — Hampshire campaigner & parish councillor

Campaigner, advocate, and a voice for those who go unheard.

I'm an English girl, born and rooted right here in Hampshire, and I feel so blessed to be surrounded by generations of my own family. Any day now, I'm about to become a mother myself.

Bringing my own child into the world has put everything into focus.

As a Christian, this is what I hold closest: I believe in strong families. I believe in strong communities. I believe in child protection. I believe in the safety of women and girls.

It is something I have given my time to for years, with qualifications in child safety and development along the way.

I love my country with all my heart. 🇬🇧
