About Sherrie

A short introduction to who Sherrie is and why she is running.

Sherrie De Kiguchi is a mother, community organiser, and long-time advocate for accountable public service. After years of watching local issues get lost between party lines, she decided to stand as an independent — answerable to constituents, not party whips.

Her approach is practical and evidence-led: listen first, publish the reasoning behind every position, and measure success by outcomes for ordinary people. She believes politics works best when it is local, transparent, and conducted in plain language.

Where Sherrie stands

Four priorities that shape every decision and vote.

1Transparent government

Publish meeting notes, voting records, and the reasoning behind every position. Constituents deserve to see how decisions get made.

2Cost of living

Targeted relief for families and small businesses, paired with structural reform on housing supply and energy costs.

3Stronger local services

Protect frontline health, education, and community safety — and hold contractors accountable for the public money they spend.

4Practical reform

No slogans, no party games. Every proposal published with its costing, evidence base, and expected outcomes.
