About Sherrie

Campaigner, advocate, and a voice for those who go unheard.

Sherrie De Kiguchi is a public advocate who has dedicated herself to tackling child sexual exploitation and demanding accountability for the institutions that failed survivors. She speaks out on the issues that too many in public life avoid — from grooming gang accountability to a broken immigration system — because she believes ordinary people deserve honest representation.

Her approach is direct and uncompromising: speak plainly, stand firm under pressure, and never back down when it matters most. Sherrie campaigns not for party advantage, but for the families and communities that have been let down for too long.

What Sherrie stands for

Four priorities at the heart of the campaign.

1Public Safety

Safer streets, stronger communities, and real accountability in policing — not just warm words.

2Justice for Victims

Ending the culture of cover-ups. Victims must be heard, believed, and protected — full stop.

3Child Protection

Zero tolerance for grooming gangs. Full accountability for every institution that failed survivors like those in Rotherham.

4Immigration

A controlled, fair immigration system that works for the people of Britain — not against them.
