About Sherrie De Kiguchi — Hampshire campaigner & parish councillor

Campaigner, advocate, and a voice for those who go unheard.

Sherrie De Kiguchi was born and raised in Hampshire, England. A proud English woman with a background in finance, she has provided frontline support to survivors of sexual violence and currently serves her community as a parish councillor. She believes in accountability, transparency, and doing what is right, not what is easy. Sherrie is about to give birth to her first child. She grew up surrounded by a big family and looks forward to creating the same warmth and connection for her own. It has deepened her conviction that Britain's strength lies in its communities, not its politicians.

Inspired by the courage of whistleblowers and survivors who have refused to stay silent, Sherrie became a vocal campaigner for child protection, support for survivors of child sexual exploitation, and accountability for the grooming gangs that have devastated towns like Rotherham, Rochdale, Telford and Oldham. She has seen first hand how institutions fail the most vulnerable when political convenience overrides moral duty.

Her approach is direct and uncompromising: speak plainly, stand firm under pressure, and never back down when it matters most. Sherrie campaigns not for party advantage, but for the families and communities across Britain that have been let down for far too long.
